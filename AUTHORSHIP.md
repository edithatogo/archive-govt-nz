# Authorship and accountability

Git history records the human or service identity that submitted each change.
Authorship claims must reflect substantive contribution and must not attribute
work to a person who did not participate.

AI systems are tools, not accountable authors or reviewers. Material assistance
is disclosed under `AI_POLICY.md`; the submitting human is responsible for
source provenance, licensing, security, correctness, tests, citations, and
publication decisions. Automated evidence is recorded as tool output, not as a
human endorsement.

## Reconciliation acceptance: evidence/advice remains an explicit blocked anti-join

A source-approved, bounded read-only evidence export or permitted API is required before comparing the two legacy indexes and any new-host records. When authorised, preserve the **exact source host, index, captured-at time, query/filter/sort scope, page or cursor, raw response hash, item permalink, source record ID, business ID, committee, Parliament, document category, release and modification dates, attachment IDs/URLs and attachment outcome** for every observed row. Record source-reported totals separately from retained pages, distinct IDs, duplicate rows and cross-index matched IDs.

1. Establish one unfiltered baseline and bounded filtered controls **per index**; verify effective scope (Parliament, committee, document type, business, date, sorting), page boundaries, total changes and terminal-page behaviour without circumventing host access policy.
2. Join **within a source identity namespace** by exact source ID. Across namespaces use only a documented explicit source cross-reference or an independently verified ID equivalence. Identical titles, dates, committee names, business titles or PDF hashes are insufficient to identify the *same parliamentary record*. The same bytes may be shared by distinct records, and one record may have multiple versions/attachments.
3. Produce a scope-labelled match ledger: evidence-only, submissions/advice-only, explicitly matched, ambiguous and unmatched; separately count filtered duplicates, withdrawn/replaced observations, metadata-only, attachment failures, unreleased/private/secret and rights-pending entries. Preserve the raw material privately when restricted; publish only authorised aggregate reconciliation statistics.
4. Reconcile the report stream by its **own** GUID namespace. No report→submission/advice join or historical completeness assertion is authorised from the existing 10,970-entry response alone. Retain per-period historical gaps and the 54th/55th UI scope discrepancy pending fresh authoritative validation.

**Current result:** no qualifying paired, access-permitted complete index snapshots or crosswalk exist in the repository. **Observed overlap = unknown**, **unique union = unknown**, **full historical coverage = unverified**. This is a blocked acceptance criterion, not a zero-overlap finding. Do not bypass robots restrictions with alternate user agents, proxies, caches or scraping services.

## Source-level reuse and privacy decisions

| Material and representation | Decision for the public HF archive | Evidence / restriction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Select committee **reports** | **Eligible**, subject to correct source identity, exact hash, source authenticity and any separable third-party components | [Parliament copyright guidance](https://www.parliament.nz/Copyright) states select committee reports are public domain; report API/PDF lane already qualified. Do not infer permission for all linked attachments or other document kinds. |
| Office of the Clerk / Parliamentary Service **owned** metadata/content | **Conditionally eligible** with applicable CC BY 4.0 attribution, source/changes and provenance; decide at the actual object level | General licence is for content the rights holder owns. It does not extend to submissions, most advice or third-party inclusions. Preserve original bytes distinct from derived catalogue rows. |
| Released **public submissions** and supplements | **Held by default** until specific licence, copyright-owner permission or other documented applicable basis for the exact source representation and intended public redistribution, plus privacy/release review | General licence explicitly excludes select committee submissions. Public parliamentary release is not a grant to mirror the material on a different public host. |
| Most **committee advice**, external departmental reports, third-party parliamentary papers | **Held by default** until an item- and rights-holder-specific basis, with checks for third-party content | General licence explicitly excludes most advice and papers. A department's separate general website licence is not necessarily a licence for a specific advice copy and all components. |
| Private, secret, withdrawn, suppressed, confidential, or otherwise restricted evidence | **Not eligible for public admission without a new, explicit authoritative release and applicable reuse/privacy basis** | Release status, privacy and later source changes must be independently determined; public index descriptions do not prove the body is released. |
| Specifically licensed *author-owned* derivatives | **Only the reviewed, hash-bound derivative/item** may be eligible, not the corresponding Parliament-held original by implication | [1 November item-level rights checkpoint](../conductor/tracks/parliament_archive_streams_20261026/plan.md) separately admitted one licensed author representation and retained seven controls on hold. No general submission rights grant. |

Parliament's [Privacy statement](https://www.parliament.nz/Privacy) warns that personal details inside submission text may be published with it; submitting contact details separately does not validate a third-party bulk mirror. The [Select committee guidance](https://www3.parliament.nz/en/pb/sc/select-committee-faqs/) describes release timing and exceptional editing/removal. For future item admission, require explicit released status, personal-information screening/minimisation, redaction/suppression and withdrawal handling, and a human-reviewed rights record bound to **source ID + bytes SHA-256 + licence/authority URL + permitted representation + destination + reviewed date**. A source URL alone, with no substantive authority, is insufficient. Keep private source payloads, raw personal information and credentials out of Git, issue comments, PRs and public evidence.

Other `parliament/` subcollections require their own item-specific rights and manifest contracts. Never overwrite maintained catalogue/card or invent blanket licences.
- Existing `NativeHub` uses public target readback with `token=False`, the retained authenticated user's identity for `HfApi.create_commit` against an observed `parent_commit`, immutable path checks, and anonymous `hf_hub_download` at the **exact returned revision** to compare manifest and original bytes. The writer serialises updates. Unknown commit outcomes require readback; they are not permission to re-send. No private/gated fallback and no repository-creation route.
- Local, uploaded, remotely hash-verified and naturally scheduled states are **separate**. PR #787 and dated P7 receipts establish bounded report pilot and hosted recovery, not rights eligibility for other sources, a complete cross-index census, or a full historical backfill.

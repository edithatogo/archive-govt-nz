# AI assistance policy

AI assistance is permitted for design, code, tests, documentation, analysis,
and maintenance when its use is transparent and independently verified.

For a material contribution, record:

- the system or service used and the kind of assistance;
- the affected files or decisions;
- the tests, source checks, and human review performed;
- unresolved uncertainty, generated-data provenance, and licensing concerns.

Never provide an AI service with credentials, personal information, signed
URLs, quarantined content, restricted source data, or unpublished security
details unless an explicit authorised environment and data-handling agreement
permit it.

AI output must not create unsupported completeness, conformance, rights,
security, upload, verification, publication, citation, or authorship claims.
Before contributing to another project, follow its current AI disclosure,
contribution, CLA/DCO, and security-reporting policies.

# Source-specific rights

This is a mixed-source archive, not a blanket-licensed compilation of external content. Project-owned code is Apache-2.0; that does not relicense archived documents.

For New Zealand Parliament reports, the [Parliament copyright statement](https://www.parliament.nz/Copyright) identifies select committee reports as public domain. Official Office of the Clerk / Parliamentary Service catalogue metadata is attributed to New Zealand Parliament under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Original API bytes are unchanged. Every eligible representation has a hash-bound basis and evidence URL in its batch manifest.

Submissions and most advice to select committees are excluded from the general source permission. They remain blocked unless specific applicable reuse evidence is qualified. Other source objects/evidence retain their own terms; inspect their source rights and provenance before reuse. Public accessibility is not itself a reuse grant. Logos, third-party content and personal information may have separate restrictions. No new rights are granted by this metadata maintenance.

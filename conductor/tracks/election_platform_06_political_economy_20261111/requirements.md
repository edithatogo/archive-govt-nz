# 06_political_economy — Should acceptance specification

## Scope
Archive campaign finances, CPI-adjusted costs, demographics, roll migration and policies; implement RDD/IV tests and SHAP as noncausal predictive attribution.

## Completion conditions
Model-specific data rights, assumptions, placebo/sensitivity checks, credible uncertainty and clear ecological inference limitations.

## Research and release contract
Observed, derived, estimated, counterfactual and experimental results must be distinguishable. Every output binds to source-vintage, software revision, model assumptions and hash. Single developer automation rather than reviewer bureaucracy. No green gate or source provenance may be inferred from a committed plan.

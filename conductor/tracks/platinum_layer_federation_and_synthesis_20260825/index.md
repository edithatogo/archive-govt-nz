## Overview

Establishes the Platinum Layer for `archive-govt-nz`, providing:
1. **Zero-Copy Cross-Repository Federation (Group 4):** Live analytical joins linking NZ legislation/gazette/health records with `global-medicines-atlas` (pricing, international regulatory approvals) and `fyi-archive` (OIA / FOI disclosures).
2. **Croissant ML Metadata Standard Integration:** Automatic generation of machine-readable `croissant.json` descriptors for Hugging Face, Kaggle, and Google Dataset Search.
3. **Citable Multi-Target Synthesis:** Publication routing, pre-reserved Zenodo DOIs, signed distribution receipts, and readback fixity verifications.

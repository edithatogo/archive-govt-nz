# Track Spec: Frontier & Beyond-Frontier Election Visualisation Engine

## Context & Objectives
To visualize the 7 November 2026 New Zealand General Election—distinguished by Labour and National dropping below 30%, a record 41.4% third-party vote, an unprecedented 11-seat overhang (National 7, Te Pāti Māori 4, expanding the House from 120 to 131 seats), the historic 7-seat entry of The Opportunity Party (TOP), and 400,000 uncounted special votes—this track establishes bleeding-edge computational graphics and complex-systems visualization engines.

## Architectures & Visual Archetypes
1. **The 'Overhang Elasticity' Topology Map**: 3D force-directed node net in Three.js/WebGL where electorate nodes tethered to winning party hubs stretch elastic bonds and warp the parliament container geometry under National's 7 overhang seats and Te Pāti Māori's 4 overhang seats.
2. **'Special Vote' Quantum Probability Clouds**: Dynamic canvas shader rendering continuous probability density fields (KDE) for the 400,000 special votes, shifting with -1.6 to -1.7 pp right-bloc decay and snapping upon observation.
3. **'Political Splintering' Hyper-Volume**: Parallel Coordinates Joyplot tracking voting booth flows across four multi-dimensional axes (2023 Vote $\rightarrow$ Urban/Rural $\rightarrow$ Candidate Vote $\rightarrow$ 2026 Party Vote) mapping the fracturing of the major party duopoly into TOP, ACT, Greens, and TPM.
4. **Bivariate Cartographic Anamorphosis**: Fluid dynamics simulation morphing true New Zealand island geography based on Voter Turnout Efficiency vs Minor Party Penetration.
5. **Multi-Party 'Coalition Matrix' Generative Trees**: Radial dendrogram with hierarchical edge bundling and semantic policy tension algorithms mapping viable coalitions to the 66-seat threshold.
6. **Autonomous Multi-Agent 'Artificial Life' Parliament (131-MP Boids Swarm)**: Browser-runnable 131-agent flocking simulation modeling individual MP reactions, negotiation huddles, and party-line breaks to policy attractors.
7. **Quantum Wave-Function Superposition & Collapse**: Interactive double-slit style quantum interference simulation modeling entangled marginal seat swings.

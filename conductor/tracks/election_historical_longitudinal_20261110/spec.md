# Track Spec: Historical Longitudinal Election Analysis (1996–2026)

Conduct a systematic historical analysis of all 10 MMP elections in New Zealand. Benchmark the occurrences of overhang seats (2005, 2008, 2011, 2023, and 2026 preliminary), examine the causes (concentrated minor party electorate support vs nationwide party vote), and analyze split-ticket voting patterns and electorate volatility.

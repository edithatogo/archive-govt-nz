# Track Spec: Campaign Finance Causal Inference & Multivariate Drivers

## Objectives
1. **Campaign Finance & Outcome Causality:**
   - Conduct rigorous econometric analysis linking Electoral Commission returns (donations, loans, candidate expenses, and third-party registered promoter expenditures) to party vote yields and candidate margin changes across cycles (1996–2026).
   - Implement Regression Discontinuity Design (RDD) on marginal electorate seats and Instrumental Variables (IV) utilizing statutory expenditure caps to estimate the true Local Average Treatment Effect (LATE) of campaign spending.
2. **Multivariate Exploratory Analysis of 2026 Splintering:**
   - Synthesize a comprehensive feature matrix combining:
     - Stats NZ 2023 Census data (housing tenure/renting ratio, median income, youth demographic ratio, Māori roll transfers).
     - Informational environment & AI media visibility (CiteCompass topical prominence).
     - Advance voting behavior (+25% surge).
   - Apply Principal Component Analysis (PCA), Elastic Net regularized regression, and SHAP (Shapley Additive exPlanations) attribution to untangle the causal drivers behind the historic 41.4% third-party vote, TOP's 7 seats, Te Pāti Māori's 9-seat sweep, and National's 0-list seat anomaly.

## News-to-data workflow

1. **Discover:** weekly review of new dated election finance/representation/governance reporting plus event-driven official-result and filing changes; archive only article metadata, canonical URL, date, outlet, topic tags and source-check receipt where permitted.
2. **Triage:** record question ID and why the topic is data-answerable vs source-pending vs not identifiable; confirm source facts directly from Electoral Commission, Stats NZ or equivalent authoritative records.
3. **Join:** select an explicit temporal grain, expected cardinality and versioned entity/geo/policy bridge from [outcomes/joins](outcomes-and-joins.md); store unmatched/duplicate/missing counts.
4. **Publish:** only an independently verified question-specific Gold dataset with linked article citation, full methodology, coverage and provenance; use a critical-news-response feature only if it can be delivered without conflating association with causation.
5. **Reassess:** when official results (expected 27 Nov), detailed E9 statistics (expected December) or 2027 finance returns arrive, revise the issue status with a new source-vintage pointer; never overwrite earlier published figures or imply complete finance coverage.
6. **Rights and privacy:** no bulk article-body reuse without licence, no personal residential-address display, no de-anonymisation of protected donors, no unaudited named-entity allegations or individual political-profiling.

## Success measures

Each issue links to a stable OID, authoritative data receipt, join test, status and (once available) public view. Target zero unsupported empirical claims, zero false donor identities, explicit freshness/source revision, and high completeness **within declared eligible source cohorts** rather than an imaginary all-data completeness score. No claim of live news monitoring or published dashboard work follows from this planning register.

# 2002 BEFU/DEFU source census observation

This slice registers five additional official resources in the historical
inventory: BEFU Additional GAAP tables and DEFU GAAP, annex/glossary, Additional
GAAP and forecast-workbook links. The register now contains 76 locators. All
30 editions remain pending complete source-family enumeration.

The [DEFU release page](https://www.treasury.govt.nz/publications/efu/december-economic-and-fiscal-update-defu-2002)
identifies the release as December Economic and Fiscal Update, issued
19 December 2002. Public browser readback confirms the four exact links and
the page's Crown Copyright/CC BY 4.0 statement. A client-specific urllib 403
and unsupported web XLS read do not establish source unavailability. The
forecast workbook's displayed 84 KB is publisher metadata, not actual byte
fixity. No payload was captured or admitted by this slice.

The [BEFU page](https://www.treasury.govt.nz/publications/efu/budget-economic-and-fiscal-update-befu-2002)
also links its separate Additional GAAP PDF. The
[machine observation](./source-census-2002-befu-defu-20261017.json) records exact
locators, web-reader period tokens, actual/forecast labels and displayed units.
The DEFU GAAP source describes the July 2002 consolidation and GST/GSF
presentation transition. Core/total Crown expense and GST-inclusive health
purchasing definitions remain separate; no cross-basis join is approved.

The publisher labels `defu02-7.pdf` as annexes and glossary, while the web
reader exposes three glossary pages, starting at printed page 155. This is a
reason-coded layout discrepancy requiring native-original verification;
the `-7` link does not establish annex-table coverage. Annex B is located in
the separate `defu02-6.pdf` reader result (PDF page 48, printed page 148),
which is the explicit source for the Table B.1 period observations. Native
original fixity and layout still require verification.

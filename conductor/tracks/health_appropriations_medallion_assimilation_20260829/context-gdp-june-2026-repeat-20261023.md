# June 2026 GDP context Silver repeat build

The exact Stats NZ June 2026 GDP workbook was re-read from its pinned Bronze
object and normalized into three clean output directories. All three manifests,
Parquet files, counts and digests match. This records a current reproducibility
check for the separately identified 2026Q2 context vintage; it does not replace
or splice the March vintage.

The replay retains 61 quarterly observations, 915 lineage rows and 2,323 cell
dispositions for `Table 1!C27:BK27`. The literal `$(million)` label and source
reference remain unchanged. The source remains context-only: currency code,
rights, annualization, denominator selection, cross-vintage comparison, further
Gold admission and publication remain unqualified or unperformed. Machine
digests and exact package paths are in
`context-gdp-june-2026-repeat-20261023.json`.

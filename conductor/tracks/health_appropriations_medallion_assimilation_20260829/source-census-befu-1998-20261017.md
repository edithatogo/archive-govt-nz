# BEFU 1998 release census

links ten PDF resources, issued 14 May 1998. Exact href and page-copyright
readback succeeded in the public browser after a web-reader timeout. The
[machine observation](./source-census-befu-1998-20261017.json) preserves all ten
links, publisher titles and separate roles. Source discovery does not verify
bytes, native layouts, item redistribution rights or whole-edition completeness.

The GAAP reader identifies the Crown reporting entity. Its statement page 5
(printed 159) uses June-year ends and `$ million`, with Actual, Previous Budget,
Estimated Actual, Forecast and Projection columns. Definition page 2 associates
estimated actual with 1997/98, forecast with 1998/99 and projections with
1999/2000 and 2000/01. The SNA reader instead identifies Central Government;
its page 4 Income and Outlay account keeps its own Budget column labels.
SNA scope is not equivalent to GAAP Crown scope. Its reader footer names 1997
despite the 1998 release context, so native verification remains necessary.
The Expenses reader's multi-row Table 4.10 header has separate DEFU/Budget
vintages and status labels; native column mapping remains unverified.

reports that Supplementary Estimates were withdrawn from the website. The
notice does not establish the exact fiscal period or object. Existing captured
1998/99 main and supplementary records, URLs and object hashes are separately
referenced; the notice changes none of those captures or rights states.

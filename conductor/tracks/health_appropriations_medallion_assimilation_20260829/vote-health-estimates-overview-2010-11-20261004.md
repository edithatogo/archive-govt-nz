# Vote Health Estimates 2010/11 overview

Treasury's nine-page Estimates PDF reports 18 monetary statements in its
“Overview of the Vote”, spanning PDF pages 2–3. This edition puts the capital
expenditure statements on page 3. The pinned Silver profile extracts only those
18 selected statements; it retains their wording, amount token, fiscal period,
source page, and lineage. Qualifiers such as “just under”, “just over”, and
“nearly” remain in each source phrase. No amounts are summed or recomputed.

The PDF was captured from Treasury's publication link as 55,369 bytes, SHA-256
`5dabf866e4f60c5fdf9df88b3fcc5b0e537652d1d6817465efb46a97c0bbe497`, with its
WARC response and fixity in the capture receipt. Treasury dates the signed
Estimates 20 May 2010 and identifies Crown Copyright CC BY 4.0; attribution is
The Treasury New Zealand. This establishes the publisher's recorded reuse
eligibility, not approval to publish this derivative. Rights on normalized
facts remain unevaluated and the build is local-only.

The adapter emits a disposition for each of the nine PDF pages. Pages 2 and 3
are partially normalized; pages 1 and 4–9 are preserved-unreviewed. Two
independent Bronze-to-Silver CLI builds produced byte-identical facts, lineage,
and dispositions. The PDF reader's non-zero-indexed Xref correction warning is
retained in the machine receipt. This increment does not establish whole-PDF
coverage, exact dollar precision, cross-edition comparability, or Gold claims.

and page-level CC BY 4.0 statement. The contents link is labelled **BEFU 2019

the publisher label or adjudicating resource redistribution rights. The

reader observations and the boundary between discovery and qualified capture.

expenses in `$ million`. Its header has 1993/94–1997/98 Actual, 1998/99 Forecast
and 1999/2000–2001/02 Projection labels. GAAP definition page 2 (printed 90)
instead calls 1998/99 estimated actual and 1999/2000 forecast, with 2000/01 and
2001/02 projections. These are separate observations: no GAAP definitions are
transplanted into the fiscal table, no numeric cells are admitted, and native
visual mapping, original fixity and ISO currency remain unqualified.

## Separate year-end dispositions

reports publication of the economic and fiscal update alongside the Budget
Policy Statement. [Parliament's historical account](https://www3.parliament.nz/mi/pb/library-research-papers/research-papers/the-budget-policy-statement/),
Appendix 1 Budget 1999 row, corroborates that date and same-day update. The
[Budget Policy Statement 1999 page](https://www.treasury.govt.nz/publications/budget-policy-statement/budget-policy-statement-1999)
is supporting evidence, not the original December update or a substitute
Health table. The original update locator, bytes, cover identity, native layout
and rights remain unresolved. This is a published-but-unlocated disposition,
not an absence or withdrawal finding.

Parliament Appendix 1 note 2 explicitly reports that no December update was
published in 1999, following the September pre-election update and November
election. This supports a historical publisher-reported nonpublication
finding, not a current-law conclusion or an exhaustive inventory of other
publications. Earlier unsuccessful discovery remains recorded in its prior
context; PREFU is not substituted for DEFU. No original URL is invented for
either year-end disposition and no retained capture is changed.

The historical register now has 93 discovery locators. Only 1999 is removed
from its missing-locator years; all 30 editions remain pending full source-family
coverage. The current review retains twelve earlier source descriptors unchanged
and adds one source-separated Health expense descriptor. Existing source census,

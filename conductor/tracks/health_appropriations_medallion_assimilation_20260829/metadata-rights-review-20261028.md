The graph checker rejects disguised licence predicates, unapproved `sameAs` /
SKOS match relationships, imported/remote JSON-LD contexts and incomplete
resource licence coverage. It performs no external reads or writes. Its low-level
helper requires authenticated evidence from its caller; the qualified packet
entry point performs source/evidence and exact-member reconstruction first.

## Corrections

`verify_candidate` verifies legacy bytes and original assertions only, and now
states that scope explicitly. Fresh parity/recovery cannot qualify absent
derivative rights: `verify_release_readiness` fails closed for candidate/v1.
The label-only inventory gate states `supplied_gate_assertions_only`.
Future candidate builds retain captured rights as `capture_asserted_eligible`,
not newly `verified_eligible`; current candidates remain partial and blocked.
Historical snapshots are immutable and retain their original labels.

The separate unfinished metadata-contract prototype is not an admitted profile.
No new partner relationship, licence, publication permission or external
mutation follows from this review. Remaining full standards qualification,
metadata/security self-review, and the Phase 7 checkpoint stay open.

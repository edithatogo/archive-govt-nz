# Remaining year-level source locators

This slice adds 38 primary locators across six editions: five BEFU 2000 files,
ten BEFU 2003 files, nine BEFU 2004 files, ten BEFU 2006 files and the two
expenditure/revenue workbooks for each of Budget 2025 and 2026. The historical
register now has 131 primary locators, with at least one for every scoped year
1997–2026. This closes year-level locator gaps only. All 30 editions remain
pending complete source-family enumeration, capture reconciliation and rights
qualification. The [machine observation](./source-census-locator-gaps-20261017.json)
preserves exact publisher titles/hrefs, reader observations and explicit gaps.

## Historical BEFU identity and measure boundaries

The [2000 release](https://www.treasury.govt.nz/publications/efu/budget-economic-and-fiscal-update-befu-2000)
was issued 15 June 2000. Its combined responsibility/economic-outlook publisher
label retains the typo “Oulook”. Four additional related policy, overview,
strategy and NZSF-model links remain discovered with content-scope review
pending; none is silently discarded or admitted as an expense-series substitute.
GAAP reader page 43 contains Annex B Table B.6 Health, with 1996/97–1998/99
Actual and 1999/2000–2003/04 Forecast labels. Its separate statement/forecast
policies do not relabel these annex columns.

The [2003 release](https://www.treasury.govt.nz/publications/efu/budget-economic-and-fiscal-update-befu-2003)
was issued 15 May 2003. Its `sumtab` publisher label is Financial Information,
but the two-page reader identifies summary tables. Its `add1` publisher label
is Summary Tables, but the 37-page reader identifies additional information.
Reader page 27 Table A.6 contains Core Crown Health Expenses, with separate
GST deduction and other eliminations, 1997/98–2001/02 Actual and
2002/03–2006/07 Forecast labels. The GAAP discussion separately describes the
July 2002 consolidation/GST/GSF transition; it is not a cross-basis bridge.

The [2004 release](https://www.treasury.govt.nz/publications/efu/budget-economic-and-fiscal-update-befu-2004)
was issued 27 May 2004. The economic-forecast workbook label names 2005 while
its href/release context names 2004; native workbook identity remains pending.
Additional fiscal information reader page 15 Table A.6 identifies Core Crown
Health Expenses after a separate GST deduction, with 1998/99–2002/03 Actual
and 2003/04–2007/08 Forecast labels. Do not equate it with gross Health or
GAAP total Crown expenses.

The [2006 release](https://www.treasury.govt.nz/publications/efu/budget-economic-and-fiscal-update-befu-2006)
has issue date 18 May 2006 and a separately displayed signed-date value of
24 February 2018; both publisher observations remain distinct. The Core Crown
Expense Tables reader page 2 Table 6.5 uses 2001–2005 Actual and 2006–2010
Forecast year tokens. Table 6.6 Health service purchasing instead has four
Actual/six Forecast columns. No status labels or June-year endpoints are
transplanted between tables. All four historical Health observations retain
literal `$ million`, with native visual mapping, original fixity and ISO
currency unqualified. No numeric cells are admitted.

## Budget 2025 and 2026

The [2025 data release](https://www.treasury.govt.nz/publications/data/budget-2025-data-estimates-appropriations-2025-26)
was issued 22 May and updated 29 May 2025. It identifies June-year actuals
2021–2024, estimated actual 2025 and budget 2026, excluding 2024/25
Supplementary Estimates. The [2026 data release](https://www.treasury.govt.nz/publications/data/budget-2026-data-estimates-appropriations-2026-27)
was issued 28 May and updated 4 June 2026. It identifies June-year actuals
2022–2025, estimated actual 2026 and budget 2027, excluding 2025/26
Supplementary Estimates. Both warn that earlier detail is not restated after
agency restructuring; tabled Estimates remain the official sources. The 2026
page also describes applying current appropriation names/scopes across workbook
years unless departments advised material changes. This is not independent
verification of unchanged historical scope.

Treasury's 2026 hrefs differ from the Budget.govt.nz URLs in the existing
captured-source census. That census's two capture identities/hashes are
referenced as existing metadata only; filenames and page context do not prove
byte-identical aliases. Page issue/update dates establish no native byte revision.
Existing captured sources and their rights states remain unchanged.

Page-level CC BY 4.0 statements remain observations, not item-level eligibility
or derivative approval. All 93 prior resource records and twelve unaffected source
descriptors are unchanged. The new review corrects Vote Health coverage wording
to the existing 59-PDF register and nine missing main-Estimates years; older
dated reviews remain unchanged. These counts describe the aggregate register,
not all separate later per-edition capture receipts. Four historical Health descriptors and two
[source-separated component-index candidates](./health-component-index-discovery-20261017.md)
are added to the dated review. There is no new capture, value replacement,
analytical policy, publication or collection mutation.

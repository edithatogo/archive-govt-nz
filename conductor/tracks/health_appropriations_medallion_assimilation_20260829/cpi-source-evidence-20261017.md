# Response-bound CPI source evidence

This offline, read-only inspector binds the June2026 CPIQ.SE9A CSV to its exact
release, DataInfo+ version159/current HTML/JSON and the observed Stats NZ copyright
policy. Seven body/WARC pairs have independent fixed pins and exact byte budgets.
`inspect_cpi_source_evidence(cas_root, warc_root)` accepts a standard SHA256-prefix
body root and a directory of WARC snapshots named by their full SHA256. It reads
regular immutable snapshots, returns metadata, and never acquires or writes data.
`encode_cpi_source_evidence` emits deterministic JSON without a clock/local paths.

The expected fourteen objects are preserved in the permanent Health archive's
`evidence/cpi-source-evidence-20261017/cas`, using the existing CAS implementation.
For replay, stage copies of the seven WARC objects from that CAS into the flat
SHA-named WARC input directory; body inputs can use the CAS's `sha256` directory.
Every staged artifact is verified again against the closed source profile. The
full native receipt, exact proof recipe and independent capture descriptor are
retained alongside the permanent CAS. Metadata/digests only are committed here.

# Requirements

## Must

- Audit the three existing Hugging Face identities at exact live revisions without creating, renaming, or deleting an identity.
- Establish non-conflicting canonical, historical, and immutable-snapshot roles with target origin and donor lineage.
- Bind coverage, canonical state roots, counts, access and source-specific rights to primary evidence.
- Fail closed when a revision lists `RIGHTS.md` but exact-revision readback disagrees.
- Make monthly reconciliation select and compare the actual canonical dataset.
- Produce a deterministic publication candidate and minimal external-action checklist when publication lacks explicit authority.
- Preserve earlier receipts and revisions through superseding evidence.

## Should

- Retain anonymous viewer/readback observations for every public identity.

## Excluded

- Zenodo identity correction, donor README/tag changes, a fourth Hugging Face identity, blanket relicensing, or unauthorized remote publication.
